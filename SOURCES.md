# Dated source and contribution audit

Retrospective publication-intake check: 3 October 2026. The v1.0 research and
review predate this check; this is not a backdated discovery search. Before the
new extensions, primary sources and their relevant assumptions were inspected.
Queries included the exact manuscript and cited titles, alternating renewal
entropy hyperexponential zero cost, and entropy production waiting-time
complete monotonicity. Bounded search, not priority proof.

- Aznagulov (2026), *Thermodynamic realizability of hidden Markov processes*,
  arXiv:2609.14623v1. https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14623v1
  Read target definitions and Sections 6, 8.5. Supplies the exact full-law
  question and q-family. Its broader positive-infimum attainment problem is
  not resolved here; its compactification claims are not used as premises.
- Skinner and Dunkel (2021), *Estimating entropy production from waiting time
  distributions*, PRL 127, 198101, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.127.198101.
  https://arxiv.org/html/2105.08681v2
  Read the waiting-time optimization argument and Supplement Section II,
  S6-S10 (HTML A6-A10). Positive equilibrium exponential mixtures are inherited,
  with the antecedent Tu credited there. Prior numerical optimization is not
  restricted in this account to a single fixed graph. The present additional
  claim is analytic closure as hidden dimensions/rates grow, not discovery
  of the equilibrium restriction.
- Ehrich (2021), *Tightest bound on hidden entropy production from partially
  observed dynamics*, JSTAT 083214, DOI 10.1088/1742-5468/ac150e.
  https://arxiv.org/html/2105.08803v2
  Read Sections 4-5: discrete-time hidden-model fitting assumes a hidden-state
  count; discussion explicitly asks whether adding states/cycles erodes the
  bound. This motivates architecture uncertainty; no CTMC renewal closure
  theorem is inferred from it.
- Nitzan, Ghosal and Bisker (2023), *Universal bounds on entropy production
  inferred from observed statistics*, PRR 5, 043251,
  DOI 10.1103/PhysRevResearch.5.043251.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.01783 (served v1, 17 pages).
  Read the optimization formulation in III.A, simpler-model comparison in
  III.D and conclusions. It constrains model statistics in a hypothesized
  topology and explores simpler alternatives numerically. The paper's
  published bibliographic record was checked separately; this reading uses
  the explicitly identified preprint version, not assumed identical pagination.
- Tu (2008), PNAS 105, 11737-11741, DOI 10.1073/pnas.0804641105.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18687900/
  Bibliography and abstract checked only; full proof NOT read. Used as the
  historical antecedent credited by Skinner-Dunkel, not a load-bearing lemma.
- Aznagulov (2026), *The spectral cost of detecting nonequilibrium at finite
  temporal resolution*, arXiv:2609.37205v1.
  https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37205v1
  Read the additive-reversibilization comparison and assumptions. This
  technique is not claimed new. Its finite-resolution inference question
  differs from the exact-law zero-cost closure here.

The analytic proof in PAPER.tex is self-contained at its substantive steps.
No verified source found in this bounded check supplies the same complete
closure/classification. That is a provisional distinction, not exhaustive
novelty or priority assurance.
